study overview. Study design and observed whole-panel results for 139 independent baijiu bottles and 11 constituents. Seven non-empty combinations of ultraviolet-visible (U), Raman (R), and near-infrared (N) spectra were compared using four model families and nested training-only selection. Under the shared P5 rule, all 15 complete-training fits for each algorithm chose U+R. The mean macro standardized MSE was 2.8-6.5% lower than with fixed U+R+N across the four algorithms. U+R was selected in 80-92% of the 75 dependent bootstrap-refit tasks per algorithm. These are cohort-specific observations; some constituents were harmed and independent external validation was not available. The 5% tolerance applies to inner validation, not to guaranteed outer-test performance. No measured values or plotted data were generated by a generative image model.
